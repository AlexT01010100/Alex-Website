CO₂ danger alerts

Know when to open a window

Every CO₂ reading is placed in one of five bands, from Good to Dangerous, based on published health guidance up to the OSHA 8-hour exposure limit. The dashboard labels the current band and draws the thresholds on the chart. At Unhealthy or above it raises a banner with advice and logs a warning.

  1. Good
  2. 800
  3. 1200
  4. 2000
  5. 5000 ppm

Hysteresis: a level drops only once CO₂ is 50 ppm below the boundary, so readings near a threshold don't flicker.

  1. 01

    Two drivers written in C

    SPI driver with the BME280's calibration and compensation math, and a termios UART driver for the MH-Z19 with framing, checksums and timeouts. Each ships as a CLI and a shared library loaded from Python.

  2. 02

    Fault tolerant

    Reopens a sensor after a failed read so unplugging either one recovers without a restart, rejects out-of-range and bad-checksum data, and prunes old readings hourly.

  3. 03

    Tested and shipped like a product

    Unit tests check the math against the Bosch datasheet and drive the UART code through a simulated sensor. CI builds with -Werror for x86_64 and aarch64 and publishes ARM64/AMD64 Docker images.
